Scope: the 30 word/IPA reference excerpts identified in docs/PHASE3_SOURCES.md
and the 40 vocabulary entries identified in docs/PHASE4_SOURCES.md. Some words
appear in both sets. Corresponding references are in src/data/syllables.ts
and src/data/language.ts.
Changes: removed segmentation spaces and enclosed IPA in slashes; retained
one long-vowel variant for ข้าว while noting the source's short variant.
Phase 4 additionally changes the tied affricate t͡ɕ to tɕ for consistency with
the app's notation, and selects the falling-tone variant of ค่ะ. Sentence IPA
is assembled from these word excerpts for teaching; connected speech is not
represented, and independently composed sentences have not been source-checked.
The source corpus is not bundled in full. These IPA excerpts retain CC BY-SA 3.0.
Japanese explanations, UI, and rule implementation were independently authored.
Source checking is not expert review or a guarantee of pronunciation quality.
